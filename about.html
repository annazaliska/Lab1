OUR STORY

Grown with care. Delivered with love.

La Fleur was founded in 2018 by Elise Moreau, a Parisian-trained florist who wanted to bring the art of the neighbourhood flower shop into the modern world — without sacrificing quality or care.

We work directly with small farms across the UK, Netherlands, Ecuador, and France to source flowers that are grown thoughtfully, picked at the right moment, and delivered in their prime.

What We Stand For

🌱

Sustainably Grown

Every flower is sourced from certified sustainable farms that prioritise soil health and reduced chemical use.

🚚

Same-Day Delivery

Flowers cut in the morning arrive at your door by evening, ensuring peak freshness on every order.

💐

Expert Arranging

Our in-house florists hand-arrange every order, selecting stems that will complement and last together.

♻️

Zero-Waste Packaging

We use fully compostable wrapping and recycled cardboard. Nothing we ship will outlast the flowers.
